How to play

Learn mode (the default): press any P button to hear that sound.

Quiz mode: press a Q button.

  • Q under a column quizzes the three targets for that visitor (start here: same whale, different echoes).
  • Q at the end of a row quizzes the three visitors for that target (same echo, different whales).
  • Q in the bottom-right corner quizzes all nine sounds.

During a quiz only the buttons in play are enabled (shaded blue). A sound plays; press the P button you think made it. Your choice turns green if right, or red with the correct answer shown in green. After 10 sounds you get a score, a letter grade and a list of the sounds you confused.

Volume runs from 0% to 400% and multiplies the sound samples, so you can boost these quiet recordings. All nine clips are normalized to the same peak so loudness doesn't give away the answer.

Headphones help. On an iPhone or iPad, make sure the ring/silent switch is not set to silent.

Suggested progression: master each column, then each row, then the full quiz.
